Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how CDSKEY (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services available at https://cdskey.com.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information you provide to us

  • Account information such as name, email address, and password.
  • Profile and settings information.
  • Support messages or other communications you send to us.

b) Information we collect automatically

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, timestamps).
  • Usage data (features used, buttons clicked, performance metrics).
  • Cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and analyse usage.

c) Payment information

Payments for our products are processed by third-party payment providers and Merchant of Record services (such as Paddle). We do not store full card numbers or bank account details. Limited billing and transaction data may be shared with us for accounting, fraud prevention, and support.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service.
  • Personalise your experience and remember your preferences.
  • Process orders, subscriptions, and billing.
  • Communicate with you about updates, security notifications, and support.
  • Monitor and protect the security and integrity of the Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Legal Bases (if you are in the EEA/UK)

Where applicable, we process personal data based on:

  • Your consent.
  • Performance of a contract (providing the Service to you).
  • Our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Service.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service (hosting, analytics, email, support).
  • Payment processors and Merchant of Record providers for billing and fraud prevention.
  • Professional advisers (lawyers, accountants) where necessary.
  • Authorities or third parties if required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Other parties in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember user preferences, and understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect certain features.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When data is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymise it.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent).
  • Port your data to another service provider.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@cdskey.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. International Transfers

We may process and store information in countries outside your country of residence. Where required by law, we implement appropriate safeguards to protect data during such transfers.

10. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at: support@cdskey.com.
