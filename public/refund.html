Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2025

This Refund Policy explains how refunds are handled for purchases made through CDSKEY (“we”, “us”, “our”) and our website https://cdskey.com.

1. General

  • CDSKEY sells digital products and subscription-based access to software tools.
  • Because our products are digital and available immediately after purchase, refunds are limited as described below.

2. Free Trials and Evaluation

If a free trial, demo, or limited plan is available, we encourage you to evaluate the Service before starting a paid plan. This helps ensure the product fits your needs before you are charged.

3. Subscription Renewals

  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled in advance.
  • You can cancel at any time from your account or by contacting support before the next billing date.
  • Cancelled subscriptions remain active until the end of the current paid period. We generally do not offer refunds for partial periods that have already started.

4. One-Time Purchases

For one-time digital purchases, refunds are normally not provided once access or download has been delivered, except where required by applicable law.

5. Exceptional Refunds

We may, at our sole discretion, consider a refund in the following situations:

  • Duplicate payment for the same product.
  • Technical issues on our side that prevent you from accessing the Service and cannot be resolved within a reasonable time.
  • Accidental purchase by a minor where the account holder contacts us promptly.

If a refund is approved, it will be processed back to the original payment method used.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us at support@cdskey.com and include:

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Order or invoice ID.
  • Date of purchase.
  • Reason for the refund request.

We may also ask you to contact our payment partner (such as Paddle) where necessary to process the refund.

7. Chargebacks

If you dispute a transaction directly with your bank or card provider (chargeback), your access to the Service may be suspended. We encourage you to contact us first so we can review and resolve any issues.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Refund Policy, please contact: support@cdskey.com.
